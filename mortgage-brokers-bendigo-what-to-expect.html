Mortgage Brokers Bendigo What to Expect Explained

The useful first call is not about collecting a rate. It is about seeing whether a broker can translate your goal into a short list of lender choices, the evidence required, and the likely sequence from estimate to settlement. Bring the job the loan must do: first home purchase, refinance, investment lending, or conditional approval before offers. Then ask the broker to state the assumptions behind any borrowing figure, including income, existing debts, deposit, property value and repayment type.

  • Ask which lenders were in scope and which were ruled out.
  • Ask whether the recommendation is fixed, variable, split, interest only or principal and interest.
  • Ask what remains conditional after pre-approval and what could trigger reassessment.

Payment, duty and disputes

Payment should be explicit. The source material says the introduction is free and Australian residential brokers are usually paid by lenders, with exceptions disclosed upfront. Partner brokers are described as holding an Australian Credit Licence or acting as credit representatives under a licensee, with best interests duty and AFCA external dispute resolution relevant. Those protections matter, but they do not replace clear written reasoning. Before proceeding, request the rationale for the preferred loan and the reasons nearby options were rejected.

Documents that make estimates reliable

A borrowing estimate is only as solid as the evidence behind it. The source guide points to payslips, bank statements, identification and current liability details as the material that reduces back and forth. For a first buyer, add deposit history and any eligible support questions. For a refinancer, add existing loan statements, repayment history and the total cost comparison. For an investor, include equity position, rental assumptions and whether cash flow is modelled on interest only or principal and interest repayments.

ASIC Moneysmart home loan guidance is a sensible baseline for comparison rates, repayments and features. Bring those basics into the call so advertised rates do not crowd out fees, offset usefulness, split options and the structure needed for your goal.

Pre-approval is conditional, not a promise for any property. Treat it as a planning window, commonly around 90 days depending on lender settings. Once an application is lodged, the source guide frames application to settlement at roughly 4 to 6 weeks depending on lender and circumstances. Ask who owns each task during that period: document collection, lender assessment, valuation where required, offer review, signing and settlement coordination.

  • Clarify when an answer is conditional, when it becomes final, and what could reopen assessment.
  • If timing is tight, nominate the tasks that depend on you and complete them early.
  • Define the goal before product selection so structure, fees and certainty are judged against the same target.

Use a written scorecard rather than memory. Rate the broker on lender breadth, disclosure quality, assumption clarity, document checklist, timeline realism and explanation of rejected options. A lower headline rate can still be a poor fit if the loan structure, fees or conditions clash with the plan. The aim is not to find a perfect product; it is to understand the evidence, the trade-offs and the next step before committing to a full application.

  1. Define the goal. State whether you are buying, refinancing, investing or seeking pre-approval before lenders are compared.
  2. Gather evidence. Collect payslips, bank statements, identification and current liability details so estimates are reliable.
  3. Test the shortlist. Ask which lenders were compared, which were excluded and why the recommended option suits your circumstances.
  4. Confirm conditions. Clarify pre-approval limits, validity, tasks you own and events that could trigger reassessment.

Common questions

Is the first meeting with a Bendigo broker free? The source guide says the introduction is free. It also says Australian residential brokers are typically paid lender commission, with exceptions disclosed upfront, so ask how payment is shown before any application starts.

How long does pre-approval last? The source guide describes pre-approval as conditional and typically around 90 days depending on the lender. Treat it as a planning tool, not final approval for every property.

What can slow application to settlement? The guide frames a typical window near 4 to 6 weeks depending on lender and circumstances. Delays can come from missing documents, valuation needs, lender assessment, offer review, signing and settlement coordination.
